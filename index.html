Flood alert & relief fund

Authorities send a flood alert to the affected localities. Residents who get it can choose to give a small amount. Every contribution goes into one public relief fund.

How the funding works

1. AlertAuthority issues a flood alert for chosen localities and sets a fund target.
2. NotifyResidents of those localities see the alert and a suggested small amount.
3. ContributeEach person gives ₹1 to ₹500, voluntarily, by UPI, card or net banking.
4. PoolAll contributions for that alert go into a single relief fund with a live total.
5. AllocateThe fund is split between relief, pumps and drains, shelters and rescue.
6. ReportReceipts and totals stay visible in the public ledger below.

How the flood risk is calculated

Forecast rainfall → runoff (SCS Curve Number) → peak flow (Rational Method, with Kirpich time of concentration) → drain capacity (Manning's equation) → risk = peak flow ÷ capacity. Low below 50%, Moderate 50–80%, High 80–100%, Severe above 100% of capacity (overflow likely). The percentage on every risk badge is peak flow as a share of drain capacity. Wet soil raises each curve number by 12%.

Informational only. Curve numbers, catchment areas and drain sizes are demo assumptions and must be calibrated against past floods before real use. For official warnings see IMD, CWC and your state disaster management authority.
